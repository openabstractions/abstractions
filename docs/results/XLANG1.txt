A job was created in Go, worked on in Go, abandoned without release, adopted in
Python, resumed from the byte Go had PROVEN rather than the byte it had written,
finished in Python, and read back in Go. The stale Go owner was refused when it
came back with its old epoch.

Neither implementation imports the other. Neither is generated from a shared
schema. The record on disk is the entire contract, and it survived the crossing
unchanged in both directions.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT YET PROVE: no bytes moved. The transfer runner does not exist,
so this exercises the record, the lease, the epoch and the adoption path — not
resume of a real partially-written file. That test comes next and it needs a
real transfer to kill in the middle of.
