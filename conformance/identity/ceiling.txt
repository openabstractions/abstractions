# What a service may know before any connection exists. Every expectation here
# is about the running platform rather than about a peer, so a driver answers
# all of it without opening a socket - which is the whole point of Ceiling: a
# service whose permission model rests on a proof this operating system cannot
# produce should fail to start, not discover it once per connection.
#
# Only the answers that are the same on all three platforms are asserted. The
# per-platform ceilings are a table in CONTRACT.md and no portable scenario can
# reach them.
ceiling
caneve
caneve code=signed
caneve user=kernel
caneve user=signed
